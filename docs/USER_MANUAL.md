# Just Train — User Manual

> Your guide to using Just Train. Written for **athletes** first; a **Trainer** section at the end
> covers the extra tools for coaching clients.
>
> Just Train answers one question on every screen: **are you moving forward?** You log your training,
> and the app turns it into a picture of your progress.

---

## Getting started

1. **Create an account** — email + password. You'll get a verification email (verifying keeps your
   account recoverable; you can start training right away).
2. **Pick your mode** on the welcome screen:
   - **Athlete** — a complete training app for you. Log sessions, track progress, build routines.
   - **Trainer** — everything the athlete has, *plus* a client roster, reports, and at-risk alerts.
   You can't switch athlete→trainer casually later, so choose the one that fits.
3. **Install it to your phone (recommended).** Just Train is a PWA — it installs like an app:
   - **iPhone:** Share → *Add to Home Screen*.
   - **Android:** the browser offers *Install*, or use the in-app prompt after your first session.
   Installed, it opens full-screen, works offline, and keeps you signed in.

## The two home surfaces

**Home (the launch screen)** is where you land. It's a single screen, no scrolling:
- a greeting,
- a **body figure** shaded by what you've trained in the last 30 days, and
- the **Train** button — your one tap to start.

Home is for *starting*. All your feedback and stats live one tap away in **My Training**.

**My Training (your dashboard)** is the progress picture:
- **Headline stats** — Streak, This week, Last session — always visible up top; swipe the carousel for more (consistency, volume, PRs, energy…).
- **Recent Sessions** — tap any to review it.
- **Volume per session** and **Volume by body part** charts — your trend and your balance.
- **Tabs:** Overview · Timeline · Baseline · PRs · Challenges.

---

## The body map

The figure on Home shows **which muscles you've been training** (last 30 days) — brighter blue = more
sessions, grey = untrained. It's a glance answer to "am I hitting everything?"

- **Tap the whole figure** → the **Body breakdown** sheet: every muscle, grouped by body part, with its
  30-day count, plus your most-trained and most-neglected at the top.
- **Tap a region** (e.g. your quads) → the sheet scoped to just that area's muscles.
- **Tap a muscle** → the exercises that work it (prime movers first), and tap one to open its details
  where you can add it to a workout.

So a cold spot on the figure turns straight into "here's how to train it."

## Logging a session

Tap **Train** (or **Train** on Home):

1. **Start** a session — blank, or **from a template**. (You can also *plan a future session* from the
   launcher — build it now, execute it later.)
2. **Add blocks and exercises.** Each block is a type (resistance, cardio, calisthenics, stretching,
   cooldown); add exercises from the library or quick-add a new one mid-session.
3. **Log your sets.** The app pre-fills targets and your last-time numbers:
   - **Resistance:** weight × reps. Set a **weight ramp** (+ per set) to auto-step the load.
   - **Cardio:** any combination of **time** (min:sec), **distance**, and **intensity**.
   - **Calisthenics:** reps *or* a timed hold. **Stretching/Cooldown:** hold time / duration.
   - **Single-limb moves** (split squats, curls…) are tracked **per side** — enter one number, both
     sides count; log an uneven L/R split when they differ.
   - **Circuits:** group exercises to perform **round-robin** (one set of each, then loop).
4. **Personal records** flash when you beat your best **Load** (heaviest) or **Volume** (weight × reps)
   for an exercise, and a chip marks the record-holding set.
5. **Rest timer** starts automatically after a set — it counts down in a banner and buzzes at zero.
6. **End the session** in one step: name it (or leave it to auto-name by date), review what you did
   (exercises, sets, volume, PRs), and — optionally — rate how you felt (energy / mobility / stress).
   Nothing is saved until you tap **Finish**, so an abandoned session stays resumable.

The active session rides along as a **pill** if you leave it — tap to jump back in. It keeps working
**offline**; your sets sync when you're back on signal.

## Exercises

The **Exercises** library:
- Filter by workout type, body part, equipment, or search by name.
- Each exercise shows a **muscle map** (which muscles it works, primary vs secondary), plus
  equipment, difficulty, and instructions.
- **Add to Workout** adds it to your open/planned session (picks the session if you have more than one).
- **Add your own** — create a custom exercise, including its muscles, so it shows up on your map too.

---

## Templates (reusable routines)

The **Templates** library lets you save and reuse session plans:
- **Build** a template (blocks + exercises + targets), or **save any session as a template**.
- **Apply** a template to start a new session pre-filled with its plan.
- **Fork** a template to make a modified copy.

## Tracking progress

- **Snapshots (Baseline tab)** — capture measurements over time (weight, body fat, resting HR, a few
  performance numbers, and how you feel). The app shows the change from your baseline.
- **Goals (Overview tab)** — statements of intent, kept as history so your whole arc is visible.
- **Personal Bests (PRs tab)** — your best estimated 1-rep-max and best volume per exercise.
- **Per-exercise progress** — open an exercise's detail to see your estimated 1RM trend and whether a
  specific lift is actually growing.
- **Challenges** — measurable goals with a deadline; progress updates automatically as you log.
- **Progress photos** — attach photos to a snapshot and compare before/after with a slider. Photos are
  **private by default**; you choose what (if anything) is shareable.

## Settings & account

**Preferences** covers your profile (name, weight unit), dashboard options (the Train button's label,
layout, widgets), and alert styling.

**Account** (in Preferences):
- **Change password** (signs out your other devices), **change sign-in email** (confirmed via a link),
  **forgot password** / reset.
- **Devices** — see where you're signed in and sign out a device, or everywhere.
- **Download my data** — a full export of everything you've logged.
- **Deactivate account** — soft-deletes it; signing back in within 30 days restores it, after which it's
  permanently purged.

---

## Offline

Just Train works without a signal once installed. You can log a whole session offline — sets queue
locally and sync automatically when you reconnect. A banner shows when you're offline or syncing.

---

## For Trainers

Everything above is your own training (your "My Training" is your personal athlete experience). As a
Trainer you also get:

- **Clients** — a roster of the people you coach. Each client has the same Overview / Timeline /
  Baseline / PRs / Challenges profile you have for yourself.
- **Plan the day** — build and run sessions for any client; multiple planned sessions can be open at once.
- **Monthly reports** — a progress email per client (goal arc + key metrics + your note), sent on demand
  or on a schedule.
- **At-risk alerts** — a heads-up when a client hasn't logged a session in a while.
- **Challenges** you set for clients, with progress tracked automatically.

Your own training lives under **My Training** (reached from the roster or your self-training tile) — it's
the identical athlete experience, so everything in this manual applies to you too.
