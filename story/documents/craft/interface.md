# The Interface — workspace (Craft KB · portable)

Status: **provisional** — may not hold; evolve or discard freely.

These are the **entry moves** — the ways you put me to work. This doc doubles as a **prompt
bank**: when you name a move (or say *"menu"* when you're between moves), I use the prompts
here to drive the next step. The move-shapes are portable — they travel to other writing
projects even when their targets (this glossary, this story) don't.

Every move composes with the rest of the Craft KB: pick a **vector**, and I lean on the
**archetypes** and the **edit protocol** from `craft-reference.md` as needed.

**Every way of speech for the whole rig** is listed in `craft/user-manual.md`.

**Default when you're unsure:** say *"menu"* and I lay these four out; or just start talking and
I'll propose the move + vector that fits and you redirect.

---

## 1 · Ideas + Vector

Hand me raw ideas and name the lens.

- **You bring:** the ideas (fragments, images, premises, "what ifs") + a **vector**.
- **I ask:** Which vector — **Ponder** (riff & expand, generate possibilities), **Bridge**
  (map how they connect, surface the gaps and contradictions), or **Render** (organize what's
  there, *no invention*)? And is there a **target** — the glossary, act zero, a story block?
- **I produce:** per vector — expansions & alternatives · a connection-map + gap list · a clean
  structure of what you already have. I **name the vector I'm in** as I go.
- **Sub-move · Collide** (within Ponder): name two narrative tokens; I force them to intersect to
  spark a metaphor or scene — candidates only, your gut vetoes. See `craft/tokens.md`.

## 2 · Act Zero  *(confirmed)*

> **The originating seed-idea, before there is a plot.** The world before the **viewer's lens**
> has arrived. The **story is what the lens witnesses** — what falls inside the frame; **Act Zero
> is what's outside the lens**: the conditions, history, and pressures that led us to *this*
> moment, the one worth witnessing. We develop it to know the ground the lens will be pointed at.

- **You bring:** the seed — the world and the forces at play before anything is being watched.
- **I ask:** What is true in this world before the lens arrives? What history and pressure led us
  to the moment worth witnessing — why *here*, why *now*? What sits just outside the frame?
- **I produce:** an **Act Zero sketch** feeding the Story KB — the off-frame ground the story's
  lens will eventually point at.

## 3 · World / Glossary

Make the world hold still by naming it.

- **You bring:** the characters / places / concepts you already know are load-bearing.
- **I ask:** Which entity type? Name the few that matter most; then per entry — one-line, role/
  significance, origin, relations. I propose `[[links]]` between them and flag what's undecided.
- **I produce:** entries in `documents/story/glossary.md` (the bible), using the entry template.

## 4 · The Knot

Find and name the central tension in an idea — the thing you've circled for years.

- **You bring:** the idea or tangle, laid out loosely.
- **I ask:** nothing yet — I **reflect back the knot(s)**: the competing forces, the unresolved
  question, the "yes, but". Then we name them and pick *one* to pull on.
- **I produce:** a **named knot** + a few threads to pull, so a vague itch becomes a workable problem.
