# Story Glossary — workspace (the Bible)

Status: skeleton

The single source of truth for **who / where / what** in the story — our DDD glossary, and the
core of the **Story KB** (`documents/story/`), which is bound to *this* story only (as opposed
to the portable Craft KB in `documents/craft/`). Grows as ideas arrive. **Canonical and yours to
edit.** KB mirror is **opt-in**; when resumed, the Story KB syncs to this project's knowledgebase.

Keep names consistent everywhere (this doc, ideas, story blocks, conversation). When an
entry relates to another, link it inline with `[[Name]]` so the web of the world is visible.

**Entry template**

```
### <Name>
- **One-line:** <what it is in a sentence>
- **Role / significance:** <why it matters to the story>
- **First appears / origin:** <where the idea came from or where it lands>
- **Related:** [[Other Name]], [[Other Name]]
- **Open questions:** <what is undecided>
```
