## How to read this

- **Zone:** this is the **garden** (② cultivated, linked idea-notes). Raw dumps live in
  `compost-heap/`; distilled canon lives in `documents/story/`.
- **Layout — Victory Gardens:** the garden is organised into **section subdirectories**
  (`body-map/`, `type-coding/`, `circuits/`, `set-targets/`) plus **`_shared/`** for cross-section
  notes that belong to no single post — yet it stays **conceptually flat**: one graph, `[[name]]`
  links resolve by **filename, not path**, so folders group without affecting linking and a note can
  move sections freely. `MAP.md` + `_TEMPLATE.md` stay at the garden root. *(The rig model, see
  `documents/README.md` and `model.json`.)*
- **Stage** is in each note's front-matter (`seed → sprout → branch → leaf`), shown inline below
  (🌱 seed · 🌿 sprout). A note's `grew-from` points back to its compost seed (one-way lineage).
