# Honest representation — the candidate spine  (cross-piece)

The spine hypothesis started as "legible at a glance." Writing the four pieces nudged it wider:
**the UI shows the true state of training, and derives or degrades safely.**

Evidence so far:
- **Body map** — [[heat-absolute-decision|absolute]] over relative (show real volume, not flattered
  emphasis); [[render-from-data-not-stored|rendered from data]].
- **Type coding** *(to garden)* — unknown type → neutral fallback, never a wrong-colour signal.
- **Circuits** *(to garden)* — round position derived from logged sets, so the readout can't lie.

Open question for the Bridge across all four: is **"legible at a glance"** the spine and honesty a
supporting motif, or is **honest representation** the spine and glanceability one of its expressions?
(Set-targets is the deliberate counterexample — it sits on the *Capture* side, sharing the values,
not the glanceability.)
