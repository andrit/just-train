# The body map is a diagnostic funnel, not a readout

The Home body figure is the **top of a funnel that ends in "train this today."** Its job isn't to
*display* what you trained — it's to surface a gap and hand you the next action: see a dim/gray
region → tap it → that region's muscles → library exercises that fill it → open one → **Add to
Workout**. The headline of the whole piece.

Two resolutions of one dataset make the funnel work: the **figure** is the coarse glance, the
**sheet** is the fine drill (and reaches muscles the SVG can't paint). Glance and drill want
different granularity, and the app gives each its own.
