# Colour meaning is contextual to the surface it's painted on

The same body geometry wears **two colour languages**: Ember Red for muscle *roles* on the
per-exercise placard ("these are the muscles this move works"), and a Command-Blue *magnitude* ramp
on the Home heat-map — **because "red on a body reads as soreness/injury."** The red that means
"worked" on a placard would mean "hurt" on *your* body.

The sophisticated beat: the app doesn't have one fixed colour meaning; meaning depends on the
surface. (Writer confirmed these read as coherent — different contexts, not confusion.)
