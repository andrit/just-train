# The gray is the signal — make absence visible

The actionable part of the heat-map is the **untrained (gray)** regions, not the bright ones.
Absence — what you're *not* training — is the hard thing to notice, so the app surfaces it as a
first-class **"Neglected · N muscles untrained"** tile rather than leaving you to infer it from dim
regions. The design points the eye at the gap on purpose.

This is why [[heat-absolute-decision|absolute heat]] matters: a relative scale would never let the
figure read as mostly-empty, which is exactly the honest signal a light month should give.
