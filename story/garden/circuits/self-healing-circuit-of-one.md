# Self-healing — a circuit of one demotes to solo

If a circuit drops to a single member (e.g. a member deleted without ungrouping the rest), it is
**demoted to a solo exercise** — the UI refuses to render a nonsensical bracketed "circuit of one."
A small fail-safe in the same family as type coding's unknown→neutral fallback: the interface won't
show a state that can't be true.
