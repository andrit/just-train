# The Ramp — compact intent-encoding

Instead of hand-typing 10 / 8 / 6, you state the **rule** — start 10, step −2 — and it expands into the
per-set values, still hand-editable after. The plan captures your *intent* compactly; the rule is a
starting point, not a cage. Same "express the structure, don't hand-enter it" idea as the circuit box
([[circuit-as-a-box]]) and the heat ladder — a tiny language for prescribing.
