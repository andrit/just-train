# Triple-redundant encoding — the real affordance

Colour for the tenth-of-a-second glance, **shape** (the glyph) for colour-blindness / greyscale /
glare, **label** for certainty. The feature isn't "category tags" — it's *recognition speed with a
safety net*. This is the type-coding form of the body map's "labelled fallback" and circuits'
"derived state": the system never relies on one channel alone.

**Doubt to vet:** is robustness really the *centre* of the feature, or my preferred angle? Most users
read the colour and move on; the glyph+label are insurance, not the daily experience.
